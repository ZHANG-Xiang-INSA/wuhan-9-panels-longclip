Wuhan photography boards

Joint width on boards 5, 6 and 8

Three boards cannot be built to the mortar width given in the proposal. The slip size and the bond decide the joint between them, and for these three bonds the only joint that works is 10 mm. This sets out what was checked before the change was made, and what each board looks like either way.

01What the proposal specifies

All nine boards use a 215 × 65 slip. The mortar column gives a width and a colour for each board, grouped in threes. Boards 5 and 6 are called at 7 mm, board 8 at 5 mm.

02Why these three cannot be built at 7 and 5 mm

Take the joint into the brick. A slip plus its joint occupies a module of

(215 + J) × (65 + J)

A herringbone closes only when the perpendicular brick spans a whole number of courses, which means the module’s proportion has to be a whole number. A weave needs its three-brick block to be square, which is the same condition. Neither depends on the size of the board.

04What was checked before changing the joint

Board size. Swept from 1350 to 1650 on both axes with the slip and the joint held. The count of bad joints runs from 114 to 168 and never reaches zero. It rises with board size, because the error is at each junction and a larger board has more of them.

Other joint widths between 5 and 10 mm. A woven bond needs whole numbers m and n with m(215 + J) = n(65 + J). Every solution whose block is small enough to repeat inside a 1.5 m board gives J = 10 exactly. The next solution is J = 5 with a block of 1540 mm, which is the whole board and therefore not a pattern.

A shorter slip. 209 mm on boards 5 and 6, and 205 mm on board 8, would hold both the bond and the mortar width. This was rejected because the 215 slip is fixed.

05Board 8, two further corrections

Blank corners. A 45° field meeting a straight border is cut into shards at the four corners. Shards below about 1300 mm² cannot be cut and fixed, so they were being discarded and the field was left open at those points. The opening is now sized to the field’s own pitch of 225/√2 = 159.1 mm and the blocks are centred on the panel, which makes all four corners the same corner. Nothing is discarded and the smallest piece on the board is 2099 mm².

Cut types. The same change takes the distinct cut shapes from 10 down to 4.
